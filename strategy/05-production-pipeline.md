# 5. Production pipeline

This is what I can and cannot produce in this workspace. Video 1 was made this
way; finished videos are in the `videos/` folder.

## What I can produce

| Element | How | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1080×1920 MP4, H.264 + AAC, 30 fps | Frames drawn in code, encoded with ffmpeg | ffmpeg is installed |
| Original animated visuals | Custom motion graphics: shapes, scale comparisons, counters, maps, illustrated icons, camera moves | Available |
| Voiceover | Open-source neural text-to-speech (Piper or Kokoro) | Working. Video 1 uses the Kokoro model (Apache 2.0 licence, commercial use allowed). |
| Word-timed captions burned in | Generated from the script and voice timing | Available |
| Music and sound effects | Synthesised from scratch for each video | Available; fully original, so no licence is needed |
| 3D character animation | Blender with MakeHuman (CC0) characters, rendered on CPU | Working (video 3). Slow: about 70 minutes of rendering per 30-second video |
| Covers, profile picture, banner | Drawn in code, exported as PNG | Available |
| Captions, descriptions, hashtags per platform | Text file in each daily package | Available |

## What I cannot do here

- **No generative image or video model.** I cannot produce photorealistic AI footage or AI-painted cartoon characters in this workspace. Visuals are designed motion graphics or 3D animation built and rendered here. If you later want AI imagery, you would need to provide access to an image or video generation service.
- **No human voice actor.** The voice is synthetic. If the test voice is not good enough for you, the upgrade is a paid voice service, or recording the scripts yourself.
- **No posting.** I cannot log in to or publish on your accounts.

If any step fails on a given day, I will say which step, deliver the closest
usable version, and not describe a video as finished when it is not.

## AI and disclosure

- YouTube's "altered content" question is about realistic content. Animated graphics do not require a yes. A synthetic voice narrating is not clearly covered; I recommend answering **yes** so nothing depends on a grey area. It does not limit reach or monetization.
- TikTok and Instagram: turn on the AI label for the same reason.
- Every script is original, every key figure carries an on-screen source, and each video has its own animation. That is what keeps the channel on the right side of the mass-production policy.

## Accuracy

One wrong number can sink a facts channel. Each script in the calendar uses
well-established figures, but before a video is rendered I re-check every number
against a primary source (NASA, USGS, peer-reviewed papers, governing bodies,
Guinness World Records) and list the sources in the delivery package. Records
that may have been broken since the calendar was written are re-checked on the
day. If a figure cannot be confirmed, the script changes or the video is dropped.
