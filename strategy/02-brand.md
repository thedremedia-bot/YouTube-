## Typography

- **Inter Display ExtraBold / Black** for hooks, numbers and captions. Open-licensed (SIL OFL), free for commercial use.
- **Inter Medium** for labels and sources.
- Numbers are always the largest thing on screen. Units sit smaller, in cyan.

## Logo and profile picture

Three concentric rings shrinking toward a single yellow dot, slightly off-centre,
on Deep Space. It reads as an orbit, a target and a lens pulling back at once,
and stays legible at 32 px. No text in the profile picture.

- Profile picture: 1080×1080 px master, safe inside a circle. Used at YouTube 800×800, TikTok 200×200 minimum, Instagram 320×320 minimum.
- YouTube banner: 2560×1440 px, with wordmark and tagline inside the central 1546×423 px safe area. Rings expanding from the left edge.
- Watermark: ring mark at 60% opacity, top-left of every video, clear of platform buttons.

## Editing style (the same in every video)

- 1080×1920, 30 fps, 18–40 seconds.
- **0.0–1.5 s:** the hook as large on-screen text with the first image already moving. No logo intro.
- One idea per scene, a visual change at least every 2–3 seconds, each change tied to a spoken word.
- Scale is shown by animation: bars growing, objects shrinking next to each other, counters running, the camera pulling back.
- Captions: two to four words at a time, ExtraBold, white with the key word in yellow, positioned in the middle third so no platform interface covers them.
- Safe zones: nothing important in the top 220 px, bottom 420 px or right 160 px.
- Transitions are zooms and pushes only. The zoom-out is the brand move, used once per video on the reveal.
- Sound: low synth bed, a rising tone into the reveal, a soft hit on the key number. All audio made in-house.
- Last 1.5 s: a line that loops back to the opening, so replays feel seamless. Follow prompts are spoken in at most one video in three.
- A small source line appears on screen for the key figure.

## Thumbnail and cover style

Shorts mostly autoplay, but covers matter on the profile grid and in search.

- Deep Space background, one object, one huge yellow number or two-word phrase.
- Series tag in a small pill at the top (for example `SCALE CHECK`), colour-coded per series.
- No faces, no arrows, no red circles.
- Instagram: design covers so the centre 1080×1440 crop works on the grid.
