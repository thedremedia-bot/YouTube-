# 3. Platform setup

I cannot create accounts or email addresses. These are the steps for you to
follow, about 60–90 minutes in total. Do them in this order, because each one
uses the one before.

## Step 0: email (10 minutes)

1. Create `zoomedout.media@gmail.com` (or the nearest free variant) at accounts.google.com. Use your real date of birth; you must be 18 or older to monetize.
2. Turn on 2-Step Verification straight away (Google Account → Security). YouTube requires it for monetization.
3. Use this address for all three platforms and nothing else.

## Step 1: YouTube (20 minutes)

1. Sign in to YouTube with the new Google account → profile icon → **Create a channel**. Name: `Zoomed Out`. Handle: first free option from the list in [02-brand.md](02-brand.md).
2. YouTube Studio → **Customization → Branding**: upload the profile picture and banner. Set the video watermark to the ring mark.
3. **Customization → Basic info**: paste the channel description, add the business email, add links to TikTok and Instagram once they exist.
4. **Settings → Channel → Basic info**: country of residence South Africa. Keywords: `facts, scale, comparison, space facts, science, did you know, size comparison`.
5. **Settings → Channel → Advanced settings**: "No, set this channel as not made for kids". Content is for a general audience; marking it for kids disables comments and personalised ads.
6. **Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility**: verify your phone number to unlock intermediate features, then complete the steps for advanced features. YPP needs these.
7. **Settings → Upload defaults**: category Education, language English, licence Standard, comments on with "hold potentially inappropriate comments for review".
8. Create six playlists, one per series.
9. At every upload: answer "not made for kids"; under **Altered content** answer honestly (see [05-production-pipeline.md](05-production-pipeline.md)); add the video to its series playlist.
10. Do not apply for AdSense until YouTube invites you to; the account is created during the YPP application.

## Step 2: TikTok (15 minutes)

1. Install TikTok → sign up **with the email**, not a personal phone number or other social login.
2. Username: the matching handle. Name: `Zoomed Out`.
3. **Settings → Account → Switch to Business Account? No.** Choose a **Creator (personal) account**. Business accounts lose access to most trending sounds and to some creator monetization features.
4. Edit profile: profile picture, bio, category Education. Add the YouTube link (the website link field unlocks at 1,000 followers for personal accounts).
5. **Settings → Privacy**: public account; comments from everyone; Duet and Stitch on (they spread videos); downloads on.
6. **Settings → Security**: turn on 2-step verification.
7. Before posting, spend 15–20 minutes over two days watching and liking science and facts videos, so the account is placed in the right interest group.
8. At every upload: turn on the **AI-generated content** label if I flag the video for it; do not add a commercial sound, the audio is already in the file.
9. Region is set by your SIM and location. Do not use a VPN to appear to be in another country; it breaks the Creator Rewards terms and can get the account closed.

## Step 3: Instagram (15 minutes)

1. Sign up with the same email. Username: matching handle. Name field: `Zoomed Out | Facts made visible` (the name field is searchable).
2. **Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account → Creator**, category Education.
3. Add profile picture, bio and a link to the YouTube channel.
4. **Settings → Sharing and remixes**: allow remixing and reel downloads.
5. **Settings → Accounts Centre**: turn on two-factor authentication. Do not link a personal Facebook profile you would mind being associated with the brand; create a Facebook Page named Zoomed Out if prompted.
6. **Professional dashboard → Monetization status**: note what it says for South Africa and tell me, since public sources disagree.
7. At every upload: share to feed as well as Reels, choose the supplied cover, and add the AI label if I flag the video.

## Publishing routine

**Volume.** Two videos a day for the first 30 days, not three. Two well-made
videos are safer under YouTube's mass-production policy and give cleaner data.
From week 3 a third daily slot opens for new videos in whichever series is
winning.

**Times** (South African time). Published studies disagree on the best hour, so
these are starting points to be replaced by your own analytics after two weeks.

| Slot | SAST | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A | 14:00 | UK lunch, US east coast waking up, SA afternoon |
| B | 20:00 | SA and UK evening, US east coast lunch |
| C (from week 3) | 01:00, scheduled | US evening |

**Use the built-in schedulers** so you can upload a whole day, or several days,
in one sitting: YouTube Studio (Schedule), TikTok on desktop (Schedule, up to 10
days ahead), Instagram (Advanced settings → Schedule) or Meta Business Suite.

**Order and spacing.** Post to YouTube first, then TikTok and Instagram. Upload
the clean file to each platform; never re-upload a download from another platform,
because watermarked reposts are suppressed.

**Per-platform differences**

| | YouTube Shorts | TikTok | Instagram Reels |
|---|---|---|---|
| Title / caption | Title up to 60 characters with the searchable phrase first; description with 1 line + sources | Hook line + question; under 150 characters | 1–2 lines + question; keywords in the first line |
| Hashtags | 3, including `#shorts` | 3–5 | 3–5 |
| Extras | Pin a comment asking the caption question; link a related video once long-form exists | Reply to good comments with a video reply when possible | Share to Story with a poll sticker |
| Length | Any up to 3 minutes; ours are 18–40 s | Same file | Same file |

**Daily time needed from you:** roughly 15–20 minutes to upload and schedule,
plus 10 minutes replying to comments in the first hour after slot B. Replying
early matters more than any hashtag.

**Never:** buy followers or views, join engagement pods, use bots, or post from
a VPN.
