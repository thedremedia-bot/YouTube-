## Script (as narrated)
A million seconds ago was less than two weeks ago. A billion seconds ago? You might not have been born. One million seconds is about eleven and a half days. One billion seconds is almost thirty-two years. Same word ending, completely different planet. And a trillion seconds? That takes you back nearly thirty-two thousand years, to when mammoths were still walking around. So next time someone says "million, billion, whatever"… it's not whatever.

## Fact check
- 1,000,000 s ÷ 86,400 = 11.57 days.
- 1,000,000,000 s ÷ 31,557,600 (seconds in a year) = 31.69 years.
- 10¹² s = 31,689 years.
- Woolly mammoths were widespread 32,000 years ago; mainland populations survived until roughly 10,000 years ago.
