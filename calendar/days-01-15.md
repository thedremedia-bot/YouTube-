# 30-day content calendar: days 1–15 (videos 1–30)

Two videos a day, 60 in total, across six series, so we can see which formats
perform. From day 15 a third daily slot opens for extra episodes of whichever
series the analytics favour; those are written after the first weekly reviews,
not in advance.

## How to read each entry

Each video lists its title, concept, hook, full script, scene beats, caption and
hashtags. **The script is the voiceover, word for word.** The parts that are the
same for every video in a series are set out once, below.

**Posting times:** video A at 14:00 SAST, video B at 20:00 SAST.

**Voice:** one consistent narrator, brisk and warm, about 165 words a minute, a
half-beat pause before every reveal.

**Platform adjustments (all videos)**

| Platform | Adjustment |
|---|---|
| YouTube Shorts | Use the title as written (keyword first). Description: the caption plus a "Sources:" line. Hashtags: the first two listed plus `#shorts`. Pin a comment repeating the caption question. |
| TikTok | Caption as written plus all hashtags. AI-content label on. Reply to top comments in the first hour. |
| Instagram Reels | Caption as written plus all hashtags; share to feed; use the supplied cover; add a Story with a poll based on the caption question. |

**Series templates: scenes and editing**

| Series | Scene structure | Editing |
|---|---|---|
| **SC · Scale Check** | Hook text over the object → familiar reference → the comparison grows on screen → zoom-out reveal → loop line | Counters run with the voice; rising tone into the reveal; key number in yellow; one zoom-out |
| **FC · Fact or Cap** | Claim on screen with FACT / CAP buttons → 1-second countdown to guess → verdict stamp → the real explanation in two beats | Tick sound on the countdown; coral stamp for CAP, green for FACT; quick push transitions |
| **TW · Time Warp** | Hook → a timeline draws left to right → two markers placed → the gap between them is measured → loop line | Timeline never leaves the screen; markers land on the beat; violet accent |
| **WI · What If** | Hook → the real thing shrinks to an everyday object → the model is built piece by piece → the surprising distance or size → loop line | Smooth scale-down morph; ruler graphics; cyan accent |
| **NG · The Numbers Game** | Hook → the sport moment as a diagram → the number counts up → comparison to something everyday → loop line | Pitch-green accent; stopwatch or speedometer graphic; no footage, players shown as icons |
| **WS · Wild Specs** | Hook → animal illustration with a spec-sheet card → three stats revealed one at a time, each bigger → loop line | Card flips for each stat; amber accent; soft hit on each stat |

Facts are re-verified against primary sources on the day each video is made.
